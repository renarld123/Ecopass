# Payment branding assets

`qr-ph-logo.svg` is the QR Ph logo vector by Bamboofirdaus, published under CC0 1.0 on Wikimedia Commons. It is used as payment-method identification, not as a payment QR code.

- Source and license: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:QR_Ph_Logo.svg
- Original asset: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/35/QR_Ph_Logo.svg

The asset is served locally so the payment selector does not depend on a third-party image request.
